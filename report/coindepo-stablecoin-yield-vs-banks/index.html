05 · Decision Framework

Quarterly Flexibility or Annual Commitment?

Do not choose based on the headline rate alone. Liquidity needs, terms, counterparty risk, and your ability to absorb losses matter.

Quarterly Example · 20%

May be worth evaluating if actual product terms support shorter commitment periods and that flexibility matters to you.

  • You may need access to funds sooner.
  • You want to reassess rates and terms more often.
  • You understand renewal rates can change.

Annual Example · 23%

May be worth evaluating only if the actual offer exists, you understand restrictions, and you can tolerate the commitment and risks.

  • You can set aside funds for the stated term.
  • You maintain separate emergency savings.
  • You have reviewed early-withdrawal and platform terms.
The risk-adjusted answer matters more than the biggest number.

CoinDepo is not a bank and its stablecoin yield is not FDIC-insured. Stablecoins can depeg, platforms can fail or restrict withdrawals, and rates and eligibility can change. A higher advertised yield is compensation for a different risk profile—not a bank deposit with a larger interest rate.

06 · Frequently Asked Questions

Before You Move Your Money.

Is CoinDepo safer than a bank?

They are different types of arrangements. CoinDepo is not a bank and stablecoin yield products are not FDIC-insured bank deposits. Review custody, counterparty, liquidity, and withdrawal risks before depositing.

Are stablecoin yield earnings taxable?

Yield or interest-like rewards may have tax consequences. The correct treatment depends on circumstances and applicable tax rules. Keep records and consult a qualified tax professional.

Can I lose money with stablecoin yield?

Yes. Risks can include platform failure, withdrawal restrictions, stablecoin depegging, operational issues, and changing terms. A dollar-pegged target does not guarantee a stable market price or principal protection.

Are the 20% and 23% rates live CoinDepo offers?

They are assumptions carried into this comparison from the source page, not independently verified live offers. Check current rates, supported assets, availability, eligibility, and terms directly with CoinDepo before making a decision.
