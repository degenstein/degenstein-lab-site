The constructive case

XRP has a recognizable role in the digital-asset market, while Ripple continues expanding its payments, custody, stablecoin and institutional businesses. XRPL development and regulated investment products can broaden access and create new avenues for liquidity.

These are real areas to monitor. The strongest thesis would combine persistent investor demand with evidence that XRP is being used economically as a bridge, liquidity or settlement asset.

The discipline test

Ripple's growth is not the same as XRP's growth. RLUSD activity is not automatically XRP demand. Tokenized assets on XRPL do not guarantee that users must hold XRP beyond the XRP needed for network fees and other specific uses.

Core rule: Measure the token's own demand—not just the size of the ecosystem around it.

03 / The First-Ever Dedicated Account

Why the Official @XRP Account Matters

“hello, world. It’s about time.”

On October 19, 2026, XRP's official X account launched under the @XRP handle. News coverage reported that the account was jointly managed by Ripple and the XRP Ledger Foundation.

The timing and simplicity of the first post attracted attention across the XRP community. The account creates a direct channel for official XRP communications rather than requiring every update to be relayed through Ripple, developers, exchanges or community commentators.

It is a notable milestone after more than a decade of XRP's existence. But investors should separate the symbolic importance of the account from unconfirmed speculation about future announcements.

05 / Company & Capital Markets

Ripple's Institutional Expansion

Regulated payments and market access

Ripple's broader strategy includes payments, custody, stablecoins and institutional infrastructure. Its reported European MiCA authorization through Luxembourg is an important regulatory milestone for its EU ambitions.

Regulatory access can make products easier for eligible institutions to evaluate, but it does not mean every service automatically uses XRP.

DriveWealth and Meritz Securities

Ripple's October announcements with DriveWealth and Meritz Securities add to its institutional expansion narrative. DriveWealth's announcement focuses on digital-asset infrastructure for global investing; Meritz's focuses on institutional custody and tokenization in Korea.

Follow implementation details, supported assets, volumes and whether XRP is actually used in the relevant transaction path.

RLUSD and institutional collateral

RLUSD is Ripple's dollar-linked stablecoin. Ripple announced Standard Chartered custody and a planned collateral integration with Ripple and LMAX Group in October 2026. These developments matter for the company's regulated stablecoin and institutional-services ambitions.

However, RLUSD is designed to maintain a dollar value; XRP is a separate volatile asset. Demand for one should not automatically be counted as demand for the other.

Real-world assets and tokenization

Tokenization aims to represent assets or financial claims on blockchain infrastructure. If tokenized financial products grow on XRPL, that may support ecosystem activity, liquidity and developer interest.

The question for XRP holders is how much of that activity requires XRP, how much is denominated in stablecoins or other assets, and whether the resulting demand is economically meaningful relative to XRP's circulating supply.

Institutional adoption is a process, not a single headline. Give more weight to live products, recurring transactions, disclosed volumes, customer retention and demonstrable XRP usage than to announcements alone.
06 / Network Utility

What Actually Creates Demand on XRPL?

Payments & settlement

XRP can be used as a bridge asset when it is selected to move value between currencies or markets. The investment question is how often it is selected and how much liquidity is required.

Transaction fees

XRPL transactions require XRP for fees. This is a real protocol-level use, but the fee amount per transaction is generally small. More transactions do not automatically imply a proportionate rise in XRP's market value.

Liquidity & exchange activity

Deeper markets can make XRP more useful for settlement and trading. Track liquidity quality, spreads, turnover and the depth available for large orders, not just the number of partnerships.

Stablecoins can help the ecosystem—and compete with XRP

Stablecoins can make on-chain payments more predictable in dollar terms and may attract users who do not want exposure to a volatile asset. They can expand the addressable market for blockchain settlement.

But stablecoins may also become the preferred asset for some transfers. Their growth should be evaluated alongside, not substituted for, evidence of XRP-specific liquidity demand.

Tokenized assets are not automatically an XRP bull case

Tokenized bonds, funds and other real-world assets can increase the usefulness of blockchain infrastructure. Yet the tokenized asset, stablecoin used for settlement, application and network token each play different roles.

A credible XRP thesis needs a clear explanation of how broader XRPL adoption translates into demand for XRP, rather than assuming that all network growth benefits the token equally.

08 / Supply & Market Structure

Supply, Escrow and Liquidity

Fixed maximum supply

XRP was created with a maximum supply of 100 billion tokens. Unlike proof-of-work assets that issue new coins through mining, XRP does not have an ongoing mining issuance schedule. Transaction fees are destroyed, though their scale should not be confused with a guaranteed material supply reduction.

Escrow is not the same as circulating supply

Ripple's escrow arrangements govern scheduled releases of XRP. Released XRP can be used, retained or placed back into escrow according to the applicable process. Investors should distinguish escrow balances, actual releases, re-locks, operational wallets and exchange balances.

What to monitor each month

  • Escrow releases and amounts returned to escrow.
  • Changes in reported XRP ETF holdings and net creations/redemptions.
  • Exchange balances, with careful attention to wallet-label limitations.
  • Spot market liquidity, spreads and depth across major venues.
  • Actual XRPL payment activity and the assets used in those transactions.
  • Whether new institutional services explicitly use XRP as a bridge or settlement asset.
09 / What Can Go Wrong

The Risks XRP Investors Should Respect

Value-capture risk

Ripple's services or XRPL activity may grow without creating proportionate demand for XRP. This is a central risk, not a minor footnote.

Market and macro risk

XRP remains a volatile crypto asset. Liquidity conditions, interest rates, risk appetite, leverage and broad market selloffs can overwhelm positive project-specific developments.

ETF flow reversals

ETF demand can change. The end of the recent 13-week positive-flow streak shows why investors should monitor rolling data instead of assuming permanent institutional accumulation.

Competition

XRP competes with stablecoins, other blockchains, bank payment networks and alternative settlement systems. Institutions may choose the route that best fits cost, regulation, liquidity and operational requirements.

Execution and adoption risk

Announced partnerships and planned integrations may take time to implement, operate at limited scale or fail to achieve the anticipated usage. Announcements are not substitutes for evidence of execution.

Custody and counterparty risk

Transferring XRP to a third-party platform introduces risks beyond price volatility, including custody, legal claims, liquidity restrictions, counterparty failure and possible partial or total loss.

11 / Separate Strategy

Holding XRP vs. Earning Interest on XRP

Holding XRP in a wallet and transferring XRP to a third-party custodial interest platform are two different decisions. A platform's advertised rate is not protocol-native XRP yield and should not be treated as part of XRP's investment thesis.

Self-custody

  • XRP price volatility.
  • Wallet security and private-key responsibility.
  • No automatic interest payment from the XRP Ledger simply for holding XRP.

Custodial interest account

  • XRP price volatility remains.
  • Additional platform and counterparty risk.
  • Terms, rates, withdrawal access and asset-use arrangements may change.
  • Potential partial or total loss of assets.
Keep the two theses separate. Evaluate XRP's utility and valuation independently from the risk and return offered by any third-party platform. A displayed APR is not a guaranteed total return, and it does not prevent XRP from falling in market value.

12 / Common Questions

XRP Research FAQ

Is the new official @XRP account bullish?

It is a notable visibility and communications milestone. It could help coordinate official updates and improve discovery, but the account's launch alone does not prove new XRP demand, a product launch or future price appreciation.

Did XRP ETF demand stop growing?

The reported weekly data for October 12–16 showed about $1.84 million in net outflows, ending a 13-week positive weekly-flow run. One negative week does not establish a long-term trend. Continue monitoring weekly and monthly data.

Does every Ripple partnership benefit XRP?

Not automatically. A partnership can benefit Ripple's business or use XRPL without requiring substantial XRP holdings. Investors should verify the specific product, assets, settlement path and source of token demand.

Does RLUSD growth automatically increase XRP's price?

No. RLUSD is a dollar-linked stablecoin and XRP is a separate volatile asset. RLUSD can increase activity in Ripple's ecosystem, but the effect on XRP depends on how the relevant products and transactions use XRP.

What is the strongest evidence for a long-term XRP thesis?

A combination of sustained investor-product demand, deeper liquidity, real payment and settlement use, growing XRPL activity, and clear evidence that XRP itself is economically required or beneficial in those flows.

Does this report predict a specific XRP price?

No. This is a research framework, not a price target or a guarantee of future performance. Crypto prices can fall sharply even when adoption narratives remain strong.
