03 · Yield mechanics

APR vs. APY: know what you're comparing.

A percentage is only useful when you know how it is calculated and whether the quoted figure includes compounding.

01

APR · Annual percentage rate

APR is a nominal annualized rate. For the simple-interest illustration below, the calculation is principal multiplied by the annual rate and the fraction of the year elapsed.

Illustrative formula
Interest = P × r × (days ÷ 365)

This formula assumes simple interest and does not include fees, changing rates, or additional deposits.

02

APY · Annual percentage yield

APY expresses an annualized effective return that accounts for compounding assumptions. The result depends on the rate and the compounding schedule.

Compounding concept
Interest can earn interest.

Compare equivalent periods and assumptions. Do not compare an APR figure directly with an APY figure as though they necessarily describe the same return.

06 · The critical risk review

Custody is not a footnote.

When assets are transferred to a centralized platform, the relationship is governed by the relevant wallet, account, and product terms—not just by the advertised rate.

What the reported wallet terms say

The source material for this review describes CoinDepo's wallet terms as permitting the custodian, where allowed by law, to pledge, sell, lend, transfer, invest, or otherwise use digital assets. The terms are also described as stating that wallet funds are not bank deposits and the service is not a banking service.

Read the current legal wording yourself and assess what it means for your assets, claims, and access to funds.

Questions to resolve before depositing

  • Who legally holds or controls the assets?
  • Can the platform lend, pledge, or otherwise reuse them?
  • What happens if the provider becomes insolvent?
  • Are withdrawal rights subject to notice, maturity, or limits?
  • Which entity and jurisdiction govern the agreement?
  • What recourse exists if access is interrupted?
Risk distinction: A statement about a custody provider or security technology does not, by itself, establish that every customer asset is insured, segregated, recoverable, or protected against every type of loss. Check the specific legal and product terms.

11 · Frequently asked questions

CoinDepo FAQ

Questions worth resolving before using a custodial crypto platform.

Is CoinDepo safe?

No custodial crypto platform should be described as simply "safe." The source material references security information, including a Fireblocks custody relationship, but users should independently review the current custody arrangement, legal structure, wallet terms, operational disclosures, and counterparty risk. A custody provider does not eliminate all risks or automatically guarantee recovery of customer assets.

Is the advertised yield guaranteed?

Do not assume a return is guaranteed. Rates can vary by asset and account type, and the applicable product and legal terms govern the arrangement. An advertised rate is not proof that a future payment is assured.

Can I lose money using a custodial interest account?

Yes. Potential risks include platform failure, counterparty exposure, liquidity restrictions, operational issues, legal uncertainty, and changes in the value or availability of assets. Stablecoins also carry risks and should not automatically be treated as equivalent to insured cash deposits.

What's the difference between APR and APY?

APR is a nominal annualized rate. APY reflects the effect of compounding over a year under specified assumptions. Compare rates only after checking the calculation method, period, compounding frequency, and applicable conditions.

Can I withdraw my assets whenever I want?

Do not assume unrestricted or immediate access. Review the current account terms for maturity dates, withdrawal procedures, processing times, restrictions, and any conditions that apply to your specific product.

Does a high stablecoin rate mean there is no market risk?

No. Even when a stablecoin is designed to track a reference currency, its value, redemption, liquidity, and underlying ecosystem can be affected by stress. A high advertised rate does not remove the platform's counterparty or custody risks.

Should I buy $COINDEPO because I use the platform?

That is a separate investment decision. Review the token's utility, liquidity, concentration, market access, and potential downside independently. Platform use does not establish that a token is fairly valued or likely to appreciate.

The Degenstein conclusion

Earn while you hold?
Understand what you're holding it with.

CoinDepo's advertised interest-account model may be relevant to people researching custodial crypto yield. But the headline rate is only one variable. Custody rights, asset-use provisions, withdrawal conditions, legal structure, liquidity, and counterparty exposure belong in the same decision.

The practical takeaway: calculate the potential return, verify the actual terms, and decide whether the risks fit your own circumstances. Do not confuse an illustrative calculation with a guaranteed outcome.
