Platform Overview

Managed Staking

FortisX offers managed staking services designed to simplify participation in supported proof-of-stake networks. Instead of requiring users to operate validator infrastructure themselves, the platform manages the staking process on behalf of participating users, subject to its published terms and supported assets.

Liquidity Pools

The platform also provides liquidity pool products intended to improve capital efficiency and liquidity across supported assets. Participation may involve different mechanics, reward structures, and risks than traditional staking, so users should review the specific terms applicable to each pool.

Platform Features

Institutional Security

FortisX states that it utilizes Fireblocks' Multi-Party Computation (MPC) technology as part of its digital asset custody infrastructure. MPC is designed to reduce the risks associated with traditional private-key management by distributing signing responsibilities rather than relying on a single key.

While security infrastructure is an important consideration, users should still review FortisX's current custody arrangements, legal documentation, and operational policies before depositing digital assets.

Portfolio Management

Unlike platforms that focus only on staking rewards, FortisX emphasizes portfolio allocation and liquidity management. The platform describes an analytics-driven approach intended to optimize participation across supported blockchain ecosystems.

Actual participation, supported assets, estimated rewards, and availability may change over time.
